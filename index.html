About EventPass

A Web-Based Event Attendance Management and Automated Certificate Generation System.

🎓 Project Purpose & Motivation

EventPass was architected to address real-world logistical bottlenecks in school assemblies, seminars, and academic workshops. Traditional paper rosters suffer from long queues, lost records, proxy sign-ins, and manual certificate creation that takes weeks.

By integrating encrypted QR codes, real-time in-browser camera scanning, and instant vector PDF certificate rendering, EventPass delivers a paperless, transparent, and immediate academic credentialing workflow.

🛠️ System Architecture & Technology

  • Frontend: Responsive HTML5, Modern CSS3 with Dark Glassmorphism, and Modular ES6+ JavaScript.
  • Relational DB Engine: 5-table relational schema matching standard MySQL (`users`, `events`, `registrations`, `attendance`, `certificates`).
  • QR Engine: Client-side cryptographic QR generation and `html5-qrcode` camera barcode detection.
  • Certificate Studio: Dynamic HTML5 Canvas rendering + `jsPDF` vector A4 landscape output.
  • Audio Feedback: Web Audio API synthesized tones for confirmed check-in and duplicate detection.
